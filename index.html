How top students do it

Small sessions. Real progress.

The plan is short on purpose: seven sessions of about half an hour, with a built-in focus timer that tells you when to take a break.

01

Active recall

Cover the answer and force your brain to pull it out. That's what the cards and quizzes do.

02

Spaced practice

A little every day. Cards you've mastered step aside so time goes to what you don't know yet.

03

Mix it up

Once a few units are done, quiz across all of them. The real exam jumps between eras.
