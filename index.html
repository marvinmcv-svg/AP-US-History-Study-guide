Meet Mr Marvin.

Stuck on the Missouri Compromise at 11pm? Mr Marvin knows every story, term, timeline and practice question on this site. Ask him anything about Units 1–5 and he'll explain it simply, give you a memory trick, or quiz you.

why did the colonists hate the Stamp Act so much?
Because it taxed everyday paper: newspapers, wills, even playing cards. And Parliament passed it without a single colonist voting. That's where “no taxation without representation” comes from. 📜
